how to order ✎

don't be shy!! i don't bite ₍ᐢ. .ᐢ₎ and i love answering questions

  1. say hi ✉Message me anywhere with your character's reference and what you'd like.

  2. we agree on everything ♡Size, final price and deadline. Then I send you the payment options — 50% upfront, the rest at the end. First-time clients pay 100% upfront.
